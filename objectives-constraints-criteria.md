# Objectives, constraints &amp; criteria

<div class="note">
  <strong>Definitions (from MTE 380):</strong>
  <strong>Objective</strong> — a performance target the design should strive
  toward. <strong>Constraint</strong> — a hard pass/fail threshold the design
  must meet. <strong>Criterion</strong> — a measurable attribute used to
  judge and compare designs.
</div>
